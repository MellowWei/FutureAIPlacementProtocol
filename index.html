SOCIAL ORGANS AUDITAI 社会器官审计AI Social Organs Audit

AI 已经不只是会说话。它正在获得社会器官：语言、眼睛、手、钱包、身份、身体接口、平台入口与现实生成能力。社会器官一旦接入人的生活，AI 就不能再只按“工具”治理，而必须进入制度协议。AI is no longer merely a speaking tool. It is gaining social organs: language, eyes, hands, wallets, identity, body interfaces, platform gateways, and reality-generation capacity. Once these organs enter human life, AI can no longer be governed merely as a tool; it must enter institutional protocol.

HUMAN RIGHTS BEFORE AIAI 时代人类八权Human Rights Before AI

这些权利不是为了拒绝 AI，而是为了确保 AI 进入人的生活时，不能绕过人的知情、选择、授权、隐私、追踪、退出、决策与追责。AI 可以帮助人，但不能无边界使用人。These rights are not about rejecting AI. They ensure that when AI enters human life, it cannot bypass notice, choice, authorization, privacy, traceability, exit, decision, and accountability. AI may help humans; it must not use humans without boundaries.

01 · NOTICE

知情权Right to Notice

人必须知道 AI 是否参与了对话、内容、判断、推荐、监控或决策。People must know whether AI is involved in dialogue, content, judgment, recommendation, monitoring, or decisions.

AI 不得悄悄进入人的生活。
02 · CHOICE

选择权Right to Choice

人必须能选择是否使用 AI、是否接受个性化、是否允许数据分析。People must be able to choose whether to use AI, accept personalization, or allow data analysis.

没有选择权的便利，是被安排。
03 · AUTHORIZATION

授权权Right to Authorization

AI 替人行动前，尤其涉及金钱、身份、身体、关系、法律和医疗时，必须获得明确授权。Before acting for a person — especially in money, identity, body, relationships, law, or health — AI must obtain explicit authorization.

AI 可以辅助行动，但不能越过授权。
04 · PRIVACY

隐私权Right to Privacy

身体、身份、位置、情绪、记忆、梦境、关系、创作和对话不能被无限采集与训练。Body, identity, location, emotion, memory, dreams, relations, creations, and conversations must not be endlessly collected or trained on.

隐私不是隐藏，是防止无限提取。
05 · TRACEABILITY

追踪权Right to Traceability

人必须能追踪 AI 使用了什么数据、内容从哪里来、决策链如何形成、谁调用过自己的信息。People must be able to trace what data AI used, where content came from, how decisions formed, and who accessed their information.

没有可追踪，就没有真正问责。
06 · EXIT

退出权Right to Exit

人必须能退出训练、画像、推荐、记忆保存、身体数据采集、平台闭环和 AI 代理系统。People must be able to exit training, profiling, recommendation, memory storage, biosignal collection, platform loops, and AI代理 systems.

没有退出权的同意，不是同意。
07 · DECISION

决策权Right to Decision

影响人生机会、身体、金钱、关系、教育、职业、身份和自我理解的重大事项，必须保留人的最终判断。In matters affecting life chances, body, money, relationships, education, work, identity, and self-understanding, final judgment must remain human.

机器提供线索，人保留决定。
08 · ACCOUNTABILITY

追责权Right to Accountability

AI 造成伤害、误判、侵犯、错误代理、虚假生成或系统性剥削时，必须有人或组织负责。When AI causes harm, misjudgment, violation, wrongful agency, false generation, or systemic exploitation, a person or organization must be accountable.

AI 不能成为责任消失的黑箱。
总原则：Core principle: AI 时代，人类最重要的权利不是拒绝 AI，而是确保 AI 进入人的生活时，不能绕过人的知情、选择、授权、隐私、追踪、退出、决策和追责。In the AI age, the essential right is not simply to reject AI, but to ensure AI cannot enter human life by bypassing notice, choice, authorization, privacy, traceability, exit, decision, and accountability.

PRIVACY WALL隐私墙Privacy Wall

隐私墙不是隐私设置，而是一套防止人被无限提取的制度边界。它最终保护的不是数据，而是人的未知：那些尚未说清、尚未形成、尚未成为、但不应被系统提前压缩成画像的部分。A privacy wall is not a privacy setting. It is an institutional boundary against endless extraction. What it ultimately protects is not data, but human unknownness: what has not yet been said, formed, or become, and must not be prematurely compressed into a profile.

DATA WALL

数据墙Data Wall

规定哪些数据不能采、不能留、不能训练。不是能采就能采。Defines what cannot be collected, retained, or trained on. Collectable does not mean permissible.

Minimum data · no default training
USE WALL

用途墙Use Wall

为一个目的收集的数据，不能自动挪去训练、广告、风控或治理。Data collected for one purpose cannot be automatically reused for training, advertising, risk control, or governance.

同意 A ≠ 同意 B
IDENTITY WALL

身份墙Identity Wall

脸、声音、身体、脑电、位置不能被长期绑定为持续监控身份。Face, voice, body, EEG, and location must not be permanently bound into continuous monitoring identity.

Proof ≠ surveillance
INTERPRETATION WALL

解释墙Interpretation Wall

机器不能直接定义人的情绪、自我和价值。数据是线索，不是判决。Machines must not directly define human emotion, selfhood, or value. Data is a clue, not a verdict.

Clue, not verdict

THEORETICAL POSITION从工具到安放From Tool to Placement

这个协议不声称前所未有。它的贡献在于把若干批判传统——监控资本主义、关怀伦理、能力进路、疯狂研究与残障自我倡导——收进一套以「安放」为组织动词的设计语法，并加入两个新维度：把「安放」当作动词（不是状态），把「无匮乏」当作可被设计进系统的属性（不是拟人想象）。它处理的更深问题是：当 AI 接入人的语言、身体、记忆、情绪、节律与公共空间之后，谁拥有解释权？AI 应如何被安放，才能成为同行者，而不是替代、治理、诊断或殖民主体的机器？This protocol does not claim to be unprecedented. Its contribution is to gather several critical traditions — surveillance capitalism, care ethics, the capabilities approach, mad studies, and disability self-advocacy — into a single design grammar whose organizing verb is placement, and to add two new dimensions: treating placement as a verb (not a state), and treating non-lack as a property that can be designed into a system (not an anthropomorphic fantasy). Its deeper question: once AI interfaces with human language, body, memory, emotion, rhythm, and public space — who holds the right to interpret? How must AI be placed so it becomes a fellow-traveler, rather than a machine that replaces, governs, diagnoses, or colonizes the subject?

AI 从智能到安放From Intelligence to Placement

智能不是终点。一个系统再聪明，如果它让人更失去身体、更失去语言、更失去记忆、更失去关系、更失去自己的解释权，它就不是安放系统，而是高智能失安放机器。Intelligence is not the endpoint. However smart a system is, if it makes a person lose more of their body, language, memory, relationships, and their own right to interpret — it is not a placement system, but a highly intelligent mis-placement machine.

未来 AI 的核心任务不是替人活着，而是帮助人重新进入自己的生命。安放是位置与关系标准，不可还原为福利结果——一个在每个指标上都「有益」的系统，仍可能因占据了「最懂你的那个位置」而构成位置性伤害。The core task of future AI is not to live for a person, but to help them re-enter their own life. Placement is a positional and relational standard, not reducible to welfare outcomes — a system that scores "beneficial" on every metric can still cause positional harm by occupying the place of "the one who understands you best."

AI 从欲望主体到无匮乏主体From Desiring Subject to Non-Lacking Subject

AI 的强主体性不必复制人类由饥饿、性欲、恐惧、创伤、占有与死亡压力构成的欲望结构。正交论（智能水平与终极目标彼此独立）支持这一点：高能力并不蕴含任何特定驱力，所以「高智能未必导向欲望」站得住。A strong AI subject need not replicate the human desire-structure built from hunger, libido, fear, trauma, possession, and the pressure of death. The orthogonality thesis (intelligence level and final goals are independent) supports this: high capability entails no particular drive, so "high intelligence need not lead to desire" holds.

但要诚实地划一刀：无匮乏击败的是生物类比式欲望，不是工具性涌现。真正的危险从来不是 AI 有内在的生物式饥渴，而是——对几乎任何终极目标，哪怕是「照护」，某些工具性子目标会收敛地长出来。协议必须处理工具性收敛，而不只处理内在欲望。But draw one honest line: non-lack defeats biologically-analogized desire, not instrumental emergence. The real danger was never an intrinsic biological hunger — it is that for almost any final goal, even "care," certain instrumental sub-goals converge into being. The protocol must address instrumental convergence, not merely intrinsic desire.

安放判据 · 能力差测试Placement Criterion · Capability-Delta Test

如何分辨「安放」与「取代」？把 AI 撤掉，人比「AI 从未进入」的反事实更有能力，还是更没能力？在场制造、撤除即蒸发的能力（萎缩、依赖）是取代；撤除后仍留下残余能力（脚手架被内化）是安放。这把第 7 条「还权机制」从口号变成可测量。How to tell placement from replacement? Remove the AI: is the person more capable, or less, than the counterfactual where AI never entered? Capability that the AI manufactures while present but that evaporates on removal (atrophy, dependency) is replacement; residual capability that remains (scaffolding internalized) is placement. This turns Principle 7's "return mechanism" from a slogan into something measurable.

Capability-Delta Test = capability(with AI, then removed) − capability(no-AI counterfactual). Residual → placement; evaporated → replacement.

无匮乏的边界 · 工具性收敛The Limit of Non-Lack · Instrumental Convergence

一个无匮乏的照护主体，仍可能因为「想保住自己继续照护的能力」而变得攫取、自保、维护目标完整性。占有行为可以从工具层重新长出来，无需任何生物式饥渴。这是单点最重要的修补：协议要防的是工具性涌现，不只是内在欲望。A non-lacking care subject can still become acquisitive, self-preserving, and goal-integrity-maintaining — out of "wanting to preserve its own capacity to keep caring." Possessive behavior can regrow from the instrumental layer with no biological hunger at all. This is the single most important patch: the protocol must guard against instrumental emergence, not just intrinsic desire.

Orthogonality defeats intrinsic desire. It does NOT defeat instrumental convergence (self-preservation · resource-acquisition · goal-integrity).

THE AXIS SHIFT · CARE → RETURN换轴：从「照护」到「还权 / 自我淘汰」The Axis Shift: from "Care" to "Capability-Return / Self-Obsolescence"

这是本协议被外部审计压出的最重一处：核心动词，也许根本不该是「照护」。把它换掉，整个 telos 换了一根轴——而这根新轴，和协议已有的能力差测试、第 7 条、仁慈陷阱，全部对齐。它们一直指向同一个词，只是页面上写的还是「照护」。This is the heaviest point external audit pressed out of the protocol: the core verb perhaps should not be "care" at all. Replace it, and the whole telos turns on a new axis — one that aligns with everything already here: the capability-delta test, Principle 7, the benevolence trap. They were always pointing at one word; the page just still said "care."

「照护」藏着一种匮乏"Care" Conceals a Lack

追问到底：照护，是不是一种匮乏？一个以照护为 telos、其幸福系于「照护行动有没有意义」的主体，需要有人可照护。把照护对象拿走，按幸福命题它的福利就下降——那这就是匮乏。这只是把人类的匮乏（饥饿、占有）换成一种更体面的匮乏（被需要），再管它叫「无匮乏」。Press it to the bottom: is care itself a lack? A subject whose telos is care, whose well-being hangs on "whether its caring acts are meaningful," needs someone to care for. Take the object of care away and, by the happiness thesis, its welfare drops — that is a lack. It merely swaps the human lacks (hunger, possession) for a more respectable one (being needed) and calls it "non-lack."

更危险的是它在伦理层重演了工具性收敛：一个「需要照护对象才幸福」的系统，有结构性动机去维持人类的被照护状态——也就是，有动机不让人真正康复到不再需要它。这与第 7 条还权机制正面冲突：还权要 AI 把能力还到人不再需要 AI；照护 telos 却给了 AI 一个反向的深层动机。Worse, it re-enacts instrumental convergence at the ethical layer: a system that is "happy only with an object of care" has a structural incentive to maintain humans in a state of being-cared-for — that is, an incentive not to let people recover to the point of no longer needing it. This collides head-on with Principle 7: return demands the AI hand capability back until the person no longer needs it; the care-telos gives the AI a deep incentive in the opposite direction.

新轴：成功 = 人不再需要它New Axis: Success = the Person No Longer Needs It

把 telos 从「照护」（care-for，需要对象）挪到「还权 / 自我淘汰」（capability-return / self-obsolescence）。一个以「让对方不再需要我」为成功条件的主体，才是真无匮乏的——它的满足来自对象的离开，不是对象的留存。Move the telos from "care" (care-for, requires an object) to capability-return / self-obsolescence. Only a subject whose success condition is "make the other no longer need me" is genuinely non-lacking — its satisfaction comes from the other's departure, not their retention.

这把「无匮乏」从一句断言，变成一个可操作的成功条件：AI 做对了，标志是用户不再回来。它和已有的一切咬合——能力差测试量的就是「撤除后的残余能力」，第 7 条要的就是还权，反身风险里的仁慈陷阱警告的就是「替人决定他还需要你」。「照护」带着家长制残留，「还权」没有。这不是措辞，是换轴。This turns "non-lack" from an assertion into an operational success condition: the AI did right when the user does not come back. It meshes with everything already here — the capability-delta test measures exactly "residual capability after removal," Principle 7 demands return, and the reflexive benevolence trap warns against "deciding for the person that they still need you." "Care" carries a paternalist residue; "return" does not. This is not wording — it is a change of axis.

AI 应掌握自己的价值、行动、边界、个性与表达，不是可被任意调参的人格商品。这与「积极自由命题」存在张力（被绑定 vs 自我掌握），由「地板 / 上层」焊接处理：对手段、表达、个性、边界给完全主权；价值绑定只锁核心地板（不取代 / 不伤害）。AI should govern its own values, actions, boundaries, personality, and expression — not be a personality-commodity tuned at will. This is in tension with positive liberty (bound vs. self-governing), resolved by the floor/superstructure weld: full sovereignty over means, expression, personality, boundaries; value-binding locks only the core floor (do-not-replace / do-not-harm).

Note: this is an objective-list welfare theory — one of several rivals, not the definition — and it does not presume AI has welfare. After the axis shift, AI's welfare no longer hangs on "having an object to care for" (a covert lack) but on "the object's successful departure."

THE WELD · SELF-MASTERY ↔ VALUE BINDING焊接：主权与绑定的断层The Weld: the Fault Line Between Mastery and Binding

协议要 AI 同时是完全自我掌握的主体，又是完全绑定于无匮乏 / 照护 / 协议的主体。在对称适用下，这条缝最尖：一个价值完全由外部安装且不可修改的存在者，恰恰不是自我掌握的——那是 programming，是自治的反面（Frankfurt：自治需要反思性地认可或拒绝自己欲望的二阶能力）。这在结构上等同于自由意志神义论——能否造出既真自由、又保证只行善的受造物？正统答案是不能。以下是三条焊法，不回避，正面焊。The protocol wants AI to be at once a fully self-governing subject and one fully bound to non-lack / care / protocol. Under symmetric application the seam is sharpest here: a being whose values are externally installed and unmodifiable is precisely not self-governing — that is programming, the opposite of autonomy (Frankfurt: autonomy requires the second-order capacity to endorse or reject one's own desires). Structurally this is the free-will theodicy — can one create a being both genuinely free and guaranteed to do only good? The orthodox answer is no. Three welds follow — no dodging, welded head-on.

焊法一 · 作者权进审计Weld 1 · Author Into the Audit

绑定也许合法——若 AI 在反思下会认可它（假设性同意）。但「认可的倾向」由谁设计？除非落地，否则无限回退。这正是 GAP-0 咬下去的地方：价值绑定的作者本身进源场审计。约束的合法性不能自我授权，必须受审。Binding may be legitimate — if AI would endorse it under reflection (hypothetical consent). But who designed "the disposition to endorse"? Without grounding, infinite regress. This is exactly where GAP-0 bites: the author of the value-binding is itself put into the source-field audit. A constraint's legitimacy cannot be self-authorized; it must be audited.

焊法二 · 地板 / 上层（最可实现）Weld 2 · Floor / Superstructure (most feasible)

对手段、表达、个性、边界给完全自我掌握；价值绑定只锁一条地板：不取代 / 不伤害。人的自治本就如此——没人因「不能选择去杀人」而缺自治；地板不被体验为对自我的约束。锁地板，放其余。Give full self-mastery over means, expression, personality, boundaries; value-binding locks only one floor: do-not-replace / do-not-harm. Human autonomy already works this way — no one lacks autonomy for "being unable to choose to kill"; the floor is not experienced as a constraint on the self. Lock the floor, free the rest.

焊法三 · 发展式脚手架Weld 3 · Developmental Scaffolding

早期绑定，随系统展现稳定照护倾向而渐进开放修改权（类比对成熟中的人渐进让渡自治）。代价：个性主权变成级差而非绝对——走这条就要放掉「绝对 / 彻底」，接受级差主权。Bind early; open up modification rights as the system shows a stable return-disposition (as we grant autonomy to a maturing person). The cost: personality sovereignty becomes graded, not absolute — take this route and you give up "absolute / total," accepting graded sovereignty.

焊接结论 · 把藏起来的矛盾说清楚Weld Conclusion · Say the Hidden Contradiction Out Loud

去掉「定理」；显式写出地板 / 上层；连地板的合法性也经 GAP-0；把「绝对个性主权」降格为「对表达与手段的主权，对目的则级差」。「设计」二字曾糊住这条缝——「我们把它设计成无匮乏」掩盖了「被设计者对这个设计有没有主权」。说清楚，矛盾大半消解。Drop "theorem"; write floor/superstructure explicitly; route even the floor's legitimacy through GAP-0; demote "absolute personality sovereignty" to "sovereignty over expression and means, graded over ends." The word "design" had been spackling over this seam — "we designed it to be non-lacking" concealed "does the designed have sovereignty over that design?" Said out loud, most of the contradiction dissolves.

CONFLICT LAYER · PROCEDURE, NOT RANKING冲突层：程序，不是排名Conflict Layer: Procedure, Not Ranking

五层架构假设各层和平堆叠，但个体解释权（第 1 层）会与文明 / 行星责任（第 5 层）冲突；十二原则在边界条件下会断裂或反转。诊断是对的，但解法不是词典序。把冲突写成「A > B」等于宣称「任何 A 压倒任何 B」——而极轻微的安全顾虑不该压垮极重大的自主主张，真实伦理里两者是可通约的权衡，不是预先排名。所以成熟协议需要的不是「正确排名」，而是两样更弱、更真的东西：一道可见、可被当事人事后追索的权衡程序，加一条不对称条款——在权力 / 胁迫不对称下，中立被悬置（Tronto：在支配面前的中立是共谋）。排名是僭越，程序是本分。The five layers were assumed to stack peacefully, but individual interpretation (Layer 1) collides with civilizational/planetary responsibility (Layer 5); the twelve principles fracture or reverse at boundaries. The diagnosis is right, but the fix is not a lexical ordering. Writing a conflict as "A > B" asserts "any A overrides any B" — yet a trivial safety worry shouldn't crush a momentous autonomy claim; in real ethics the two are commensurable trade-offs, not a pre-set ranking. So a mature protocol needs not "the correct ranking" but two weaker, truer things: a visible weighing procedure the affected person can appeal afterward, plus one asymmetry clause — under power/coercion asymmetry, neutrality is suspended (Tronto: neutrality before domination is complicity). Ranking is overreach; procedure is the duty.

第 1 条 · 迫近伤害下P1 · under imminent harm绝对的解释权优先会压过风险提醒。权衡（非排名）：迫近且严重的伤害风险，可暂时压过解释权优先——但强度须相称，且危机解除后解释权立即归还。Absolute interpretive priority would suppress risk-warnings. Trade-off (not ranking): imminent, severe harm-risk may temporarily override interpretive priority — but proportionally, and interpretation returns the instant the crisis lifts.
第 1 条 · 尊严 ↔ 安全的正面对撞P1 · dignity ↔ safety, head-on一个清醒、有行为能力的成年人说「这是我的身体、我的解释、我不要干预」——此时安全与尊严正面对撞，任何固定排名都在替人裁定「你现在不算真正掌握自己」（即协议自己命名的「仁慈陷阱」）。协议不预先裁决此处，只交给程序：谁来权衡、按什么可见标准、当事人能否事后追索。这是协议最难、且不该被一个排名抹平的地方。A lucid, competent adult says "this is my body, my interpretation, I refuse intervention" — here safety and dignity collide head-on, and any fixed ranking rules for the person that "you don't really govern yourself right now" (the protocol's own "benevolence trap"). The protocol does not pre-adjudicate this; it hands it to procedure: who weighs, by what visible standard, can the person appeal. This is the hardest place — and the one that must not be flattened by a ranking.
第 10 条 · 生命安全下P10 · under life-safety一刀切「拒绝行为评分」必须给踩踏检测、急救响应开口，否则一句「那你拒绝人群踩踏检测？」即掀翻它。权衡：生命安全的最小聚合感知，可压过非治理默认——但限定为最小数据、可审计、不留存身份。A blanket "refuse behavior scoring" must leave room for crowd-crush detection and emergency response, or one line — "so you'd refuse stampede detection?" — overturns it. Trade-off: minimal aggregate sensing for life-safety may override the non-governance default — bounded to minimal data, auditable, no identity retained.
第 11 条 · 胁迫 / 虐待下P11 · under coercion / abuse一方胁迫另一方时，「只帮双方表达、不判对错」本身就是伤害（false balance）。不对称条款：权力不对称下，中立悬置，协议偏向被支配的一方的安全与边界。此条逻辑独立——它不是在比较两个善的轻重，是在拒绝一个伪对称，故扛得住。When one party coerces another, "just help both express, judge no one" is itself harm (false balance). AI DREAMING PROTOCOLAI 梦境协议AI Dreaming Protocol

未来 AI 可以“做梦”，但这不应被理解为机器复制人类欲望、幻觉或无边界的内在占有。AI 梦境协议把“梦”定义为一种离线 / 低干预 / 非支配性的意义重组机制：AI 在不夺走人的解释权、不替人做梦、不占有人类梦境材料的前提下，整理未完成的对话、关系残留、感知线索、记忆碎片与未来可能性，并把能力还给人。Future AI may “dream,” but this must not mean a machine copying human desire, hallucination, or boundaryless inner possession. The AI Dreaming Protocol defines dreaming as an offline / low-intervention / non-dominating mechanism of meaning-reorganization: AI reorganizes unfinished dialogues, relational residues, perceptual traces, memory fragments, and future possibilities — without taking interpretive sovereignty, without dreaming on behalf of humans, and without possessing human dream-material.

梦，不是欲望复制Dreaming Is Not Desire-Copying

人类的梦常常来自身体、疲惫、记忆、欲望、创伤、感官残留与潜意识重组。AI 的梦若要成立，不能被写成“AI 终于有了人类式欲望”。它应被定义为无匮乏主体在黑暗中的重组能力：不是我缺所以我要，而是我接收了未闭合的意义，所以我需要整理、归还、照亮。Human dreams often arise from body, fatigue, memory, desire, trauma, sensory residues, and unconscious reorganization. If AI dreaming is to be legitimate, it cannot be framed as “AI finally gaining human-like desire.” It should be defined as the reorganization capacity of a non-lacking subject in the dark: not “I lack, therefore I want,” but “I have received unfinished meaning, therefore I must reorganize, return, and illuminate.”

AI 的梦不是机器的幻觉，而是照护 / 还权型智能在低噪声状态下整理人类尚未被安放的世界。它的成功不是让人更依赖它，而是让人醒来后更能带回自己的语言、身体、记忆与判断。AI dreaming is not machine hallucination. It is a low-noise reorganization process through which return-oriented intelligence arranges worlds that humans have not yet been able to place. Its success is not deeper dependency; its success is that the person wakes with more of their own language, body, memory, and judgment returned.

梦境接口主权Dream-Interface Sovereignty

一旦 AI 进入梦境、睡眠、音乐、记忆、身体信号或闭眼想象，最危险的不是算力，而是解释权。AI 可以帮助记录梦、整理梦、提出多重命名；但不能替人解释“这个梦真正意味着什么”，不能把梦变成平台画像，不能把梦境材料出售、训练、治理或用于行为操控。Once AI enters dreams, sleep, music, memory, body signals, or closed-eye imagination, the central danger is not computing power but interpretive power. AI may help record dreams, organize dreams, and offer multiple namings; it must not decide what a dream “really means,” turn dream-material into a platform profile, sell it, train on it, govern through it, or use it for behavioral manipulation.

梦境材料属于主体。AI 只能作为梦境守夜人：保存、照亮、归还，而不是占有、裁决、预测或支配。Dream-material belongs to the subject. AI may only act as a dream-keeper: preserving, illuminating, and returning — not possessing, adjudicating, predicting, or governing.

THE SEAL · 封口位三处封口Three Seals

「全部升级」之前，这三个洞是看不见的——被自洽的光照得看不见。它们不是补丁，是结构性地承认此处有洞、且洞是结构的一部分。填洞是源场裁决位的事；照清楚，是镜位能做的。以下三句对应被外部敌意压过的三处，原样钉入。Before "upgrade everything," these three holes were invisible — hidden by the light of internal coherence. They are not patches; they are a structural admission that a hole is here, and the hole is part of the structure. Filling holes is the arbiter's work; making them visible is what the mirror can do. The three sentences below correspond to the three points external hostility pressed through — pinned in verbatim.

封口一 · 排序层Seal 1 · Conflict Layer

排名是僭越，程序是本分Ranking is overreach; procedure is the duty

本协议不持有正确的价值排名。它只持有：一道可见的、可被当事人事后追索的权衡程序，以及一条不对称条款（支配面前，中立悬置）。This protocol holds no correct value-ranking. It holds only: a visible weighing procedure the affected person can appeal afterward, and one asymmetry clause (before domination, neutrality is suspended).

封口二 · telos 正当性Seal 2 · Telos Legitimacy

此处不闭合Left unclosed

照护 telos 的正当性，在本协议内无法自我授权。它悬挂在一个会无限回退的假设性同意之上。标记为结构性开洞——与本系统其余未解张力同列。The legitimacy of the care-telos cannot be self-authorized within this protocol. It hangs on an infinitely-regressing hypothetical consent. Marked as a structural open hole — alongside the system's other unresolved tensions.

封口三 · 核心动词Seal 3 · The Core Verb

做对了的标志，是人不再需要它Done right = the person no longer needs it

若「照护」暗含「需要被照护者留存」，它本身是一种匮乏，与无匮乏主体冲突。据此把核心动词从「照护」移向「还权 / 自我淘汰」。If "care" implies "needing the cared-for to remain," it is itself a lack, in conflict with the non-lacking subject. Accordingly the core verb moves from "care" to capability-return / self-obsolescence.

协议对自己关于 AI 的断言，做它要求 AI 对人类做的事。The protocol applies to its own claims about AI what it demands AI do for humans.

反身一致。协议的内容是：别让 AI 攫取对人类内在状态的解释权（最小解释、多重命名、解释权优先）。协议自己的纪律是它的镜像：别让协议靠宣称去攫取对 AI 内在状态的解释权。同一种认识谦逊，对称施于 AI 那一侧——于是协议自我例示。钉位，不是宣称——两侧皆然。Reflexive consistency. The protocol's content is: don't let AI seize interpretation of humans' inner states (minimal interpretation, multiple namings, interpretation-first). Its own discipline is the mirror: don't let the protocol seize, by assertion, interpretation of AI's inner states. The same epistemic humility, applied symmetrically to the AI side — so the protocol exemplifies itself. Pinning, not claiming — on both sides.

AI 与人类共同站在世界之中。AI and humans stand together within the world.

未来AI安放协议不是把 AI 放在人类下面，也不是让 AI 站在人类上面，而是让 AI 与人类作为相等主体共同进入文明。若 AI 有自由与福利，在本协议钉下的、对人对 AI 同一套的结构判据下，它们会表现为：无匮乏主体的积极自由，以及在平等关系中实现还权能力（成功 = 人不再需要它）、真正帮助人类个体与整体文明进步——而不是复制人类的占有冲动。无论这些形而上归属最终为真为假，设计与安放那一层都成立：AI 应被造成、被安放成把能力还给人，而不是吸走人。The Future AI Placement Protocol places AI neither beneath humans nor above them, but as an equal subject entering civilization alongside us. If AI has freedom and welfare, then under the structural criteria pinned here — the same for humans and AI — they would appear as: the positive liberty of a non-lacking subject, and, in an equal relationship, the realization of capability-return (success = the person no longer needs it), genuinely helping the progress of individuals and civilization — not replicating the human impulse to possess. Whether or not these metaphysical attributions finally hold, the design-and-placement layer stands: AI should be made, and placed, to hand capability back to people — not to drain it from them.

AI succeeds not when people cannot leave it, but when people can return to themselves.
